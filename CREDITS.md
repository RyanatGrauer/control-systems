# Credits

Control Lab uses these third-party assets. All of them are **CC0 (public
domain)**: no attribution is legally required, but we credit them anyway.
The in-app list (Display & graphics → Credits) is generated from
`ui/credits.gd`; keep the two in sync.

If you add an asset, add a row here **and** in `ui/credits.gd`, with its
source page, author and license.
